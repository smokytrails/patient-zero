## 1. What is Monte Carlo Simulation?
- we know that different seeds produce different outcomes despite the parameters all staying the same
- instead of reporting one outcome, Monte Carlo simulation allows us to estimate the expected outbreak size

    $X_{mean}=(1/n)\Sigma X_i$
- here, $X_{mean}$ is an estimate, and not a guarantee that a future outbreak will infect exactly that many individuals
- our 100 runs will make the estimate more precise, but not necessarily more accurate
- the Law of Large Numbers tells us that the sample mean converges toward the population's expected value as the number of independent observations increases
